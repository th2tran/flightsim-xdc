PILOT HANDBOOK / 01

Know your aircraft.

A hands-on flight model. Optional autopilot, no invisible rails.

01 / FLIGHT CONTROLS

↓ / S Pull up · ↑ / W Push nose down
← → / A D Roll · Q E Rudder
Fly-by-wire: release the stick to hold the current flight path · [ / ] Trim held path 1° down / up · \ Level flight path
Shift Add thrust · Ctrl Reduce thrust
G Gear · L Flaps · B Hold brakes
Z Radar range (Shift+Z back) · , / . Time compression (AP only) · V Camera · P / Esc Pause

Touch: drag the stick down to pitch up; sideways to bank. Use the throttle slider, yaw pedals, and weapon buttons. All controls support simultaneous touches.

H Capture heading / altitude / speed, or disconnect AP. R Wings level and zero vertical speed. T Stall recovery: unload wing, level wings, full thrust, then wings-level hold after airflow and speed recover. An engaged AP enters stall recovery automatically. Manual flight input disconnects AP; weapons do not. AP settings beside the callsign also provide Auto Takeoff, Return & Land, and Go Around. While AP flies, . or the ▶▶ button speeds time up to 16× (, slows down); it returns to real time for missiles, hostiles within 8 km, stall recovery, flare and touchdown, or any manual input. Supervise all automatic modes.

02 / ENGAGEMENT

Tab Cycle bandits. Keep the selected target within 9° of the nose and under 6.5 km for 1.8 s to lock. M Launch a heat-seeker. Space Hold the 20 mm cannon; effective inside 1.5 km. Lead a turning target.

F Release two flares. When a missile is within 1.6 km, flares can decoy it. Turn hard to separate from the flare cloud. Countermeasures are limited.
Network campaign: other players appear by name. Co-op wingmen show in blue and cannot be targeted; in competitive mode every jet is fair game.

03 / TAKEOFF & RECOVERY

Runway 36 points north (000°), 18 south (180°). From the runway: brakes off, full thrust, ease back at 150–170 kt. Retract gear and flaps after a positive climb. Or select Auto Takeoff from an aligned runway start: AP manages rotation, configuration and climb, then captures hold.

Return & Land flies an extended circuit, selects runway 36/18, aligns, descends on a 4.5° terrain-clearing approach at 175 kt, configures gear/flaps, flares and brakes. An unstable approach triggers a go-around; the AP panel also has a Go Around button. Manual gear/flap changes disconnect these automatic modes.

For manual landing, approach at 160–190 kt with gear/flaps down and ensure terrain clearance. Touch down inside the 76 × 3200 m runway, under 5.5 m/s sink, bank under 10°, nose between −4° and +17°. Brake after touchdown. Automatic landing does not guarantee safety under hostile damage, fuel exhaustion or extreme initial conditions.

04 / READ THE SKY

The pitch ladder and bank arc show attitude. The small winged circle is your velocity vector, not your nose. Speed is true airspeed in knots; altitude is MSL feet; R is height above terrain. Radar is aircraft-relative, nose up, 6 km radius.

Pulling high angle of attack stalls the wing. Lower the nose and add thrust to recover. Banking redirects lift: coordinate with rudder and back pressure. Fuel, drag, G loading, and airframe damage matter.
